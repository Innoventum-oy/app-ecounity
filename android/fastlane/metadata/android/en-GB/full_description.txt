EcoUnity is a free sustainability learning app for students and teachers.

The app brings together Sustainable Development Goal learning content, short resources, native comics, quizzes, reflections, practical challenges, local progress tracking and badges in one mobile experience.

Use EcoUnity to:

• Explore Sustainable Development Goals-aligned learning modules
• Read and watch short educational resources
• Follow interactive comic stories and make decisions
• Complete quizzes, reflections and classroom challenges
• Track learning progress locally
• Earn badges as activities are completed
• Switch between supported project languages

EcoUnity is designed for inclusive, classroom-friendly sustainability education and practical action. Content is loaded from the EcoUnity project platform so learning materials can be updated over time without requiring a new app release.

EcoUnity is created as part of an Erasmus+ funded project.
